# SR 221 – Roles Management Code
*(RMC)*

*The Director of Nirleka Studios,*

based on Article 24 of the Server Charter (SR 101),

decrees:

## Section 1: General Provisions

### Art. 1 Purpose
This Code regulates the giving, removing, permissions, and general administration of roles.

### Art. 2 Roles
In this Code, Roles strictly means the native Discord server roles feature.

### Art. 3 Administration of Roles
<sup>1</sup> The administration of Server roles is restricted by law.

<sup>2</sup> A person must not perform any administration of Server roles if no law explicitly permits it.

### Art. 4 Staff
A Staff is any:
a. members of Administration, including their appointed staff; and
b. members appointed for server management. 

### Art. 5 Role IDs
<sup>1</sup> Any reference to specific Server Roles is identified by the Role's name and their Discord Role ID specified in Annex 1.

<sup>2</sup> If a Role is not mentioned in Annex 1, the lowest Server Role of the same exact name is referred.

### Art. 6 Banished Members
Members who are Banished to The Void are excluded from any provisions permitting or requiring the giving of Roles to them.

## Section 2: Specific Roles

### Art. 7 Member Role
Staffs must give the Member Role to any members of the Server.

### Art. 8 Archive Access Role
Staffs may give any member the Archive Access Role upon the member's request.

### Art. 9 Void Roles
<sup>1</sup> The administration of the Banished to The Void Role must follow procedures outlined by law.

<sup>2</sup> The Director and Head Administrator have the power to give and remove the Void Observer Role to anyone.

### Art. 10 Notification Roles
<sup>1</sup> The following roles may only be given or removed if the member explicitly requests so:
a. Dev Blog Ping;
b. Update Ping.

<sup>2</sup> Members may receive the roles specified in paragraph 1 through the Discord Onboarding feature.

### Art. 11 Developer Roles
The Director and Head Administrator have the power to give and remove the developer roles defined in Annex 1.

## Section 3: Administrative Roles

### Art. 12 Administrative Roles
A member who is lawfully appointed to their position under Section 2 of the Server Charter (SR 101) and maintains the position must receive their position's role.
