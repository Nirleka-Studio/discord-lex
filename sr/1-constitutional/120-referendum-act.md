# SR 120 — The Referendum Act

> **Enactment Notice:** Enacted under Chapter 4 of the Server Charter (SR 100). Applies to all members of the server.

---

### Art. 1 Definitions
In this Act:\
a. *active voter* means a member of the server who have cast a vote or interacted with the server in any way in the last 90 days.

### Art. 2 A referendum
<sup>1</sup> Any members of Administration may call a referendum in order to submit a proposed rule change to a vote of the general membership.

<sup>2</sup> Once a referendum is completed, and a majority (50% + 1) of all active voters are in favour of an option, it shall be legally binding. No law shall contradict the referendum.

<sup>3</sup> An option such as 'Abstain' is exempt from paragraph 2 and if a majority are in favor of this option, it shall have no binding effect.

<sup>4</sup> Laws shall be changed immediately to conform to the referendum.

### Art. 3 Voting eligibility
<sup>1</sup> All members of the server shall have the right and are obligated to vote on any referendums by the Administration.

<sup>2</sup> Those who are Banished to The Void are exempt from paragraph 1.

### Art. 4 Viewability
A referendum shall be visible to all members of the server, excluding those who are banished.

### Art. 5 Minimum duration
A referendum shall last at a minimum of three (3) days.

### Art. 6 Documentation
<sup>1</sup> The Director or an appointed worker shall document all referendums.

<sup>2</sup> Starting from 12 Aug. 2026, all referendums shall be numbered sequentially and in the format of `Referendum No. X`.
