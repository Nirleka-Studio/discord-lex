# SR 555 — The Culture Code Between the Director and Members of the Nirleka Studios Discord Server

> **Enactment Notice:** Enacted in accordance to Chapter 4 of the Server Charter (SR 100), by the Director

---

### Art. 1 Cat Supremacy
The Cat is our Soupurreme Glorious Leadah.

### Art. 2 The Director
The Director possesses Soupurreme authority delegated by The Cat.

### Art. 3 Anonymity
Everyone shall have the right to anonymity.

### Art. 4 Humanity
Do not be a dick.

### Art. 5 Nuclear power
Nuclear power plants are among the most overcomplicated ways to boil water. Opposing nuclear power is an opposition against The Cat, and is blasphemy.

### Art. 6 Patience
And whoever is patient and forgives—indeed, that is of the matters requiring determination.

### Art. 7 Trolling
Trolling, or in the current era that is also referred to as rage-baiting, shall not be taken seriously. It means they win. Follow Art. 6.

### Art. 8 Balance
<sup>1</sup> Everyone is equally worthless.

<sup>2</sup> Everyone is made from the hands of a single Creator.

<sup>3</sup> A mortal asserting superiority against another mortal on their natural characteristics is transgression against The Cat.

<sup>4</sup> Male and female, female and male, one needs another. One serves a specific purpose. Otherwise, humanity can not exist. To assert superiority over sex is transgression against The Cat.

### Art. 9 A Belief
<sup>1</sup> There is no compulsion in religion.

<sup>2</sup> The power to punish a mortal for a transgression against the Creator rests between the mortal and the Creator.

<sup>3</sup> Surely someone who believes that our Creator smiles on and rewards the deliberate killing and maiming of innocents believes in a cruel creator. That is not, it cannot be, the Creator of Humanity.

<sup>4</sup> Can a mortal truly follow the words of the Creator? Does one mortal's transgression stain the words of the Creator?
