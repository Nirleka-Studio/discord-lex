# SR 510 — The Dokusai-kun and The Server

*The Director of Nirleka Studios,*

based on Article 33 of the Server Charter[^1],

*decrees:*

## Art. 1 Purpose
Dokusai-kun is the symbol and Dicktator of the server and its unity.

## Art. 2 Authority
Dokusai-kun remains under the law and the Administration.

## Art. 3 Power
Dokusai-kun may exercise any power that the Administration does not restrict.

## Art. 4 Restriction on Powers
<sup>1</sup> Dokusai-kun must not give roles to members that holds administrative permissions.

<sup>2</sup> Dokusai-kun must not give any currency to another member or to themselves.

<sup>3</sup> Dokusai-kun must not delete a channel unilaterally.

## Art. 5 Passing the Crown
<sup>1</sup> If Dokusai-kun did not send a message or done an interaction in the last 90-days, the People may do a server-wide vote to replace Dokusai-kun with another member.

<sup>2</sup> Candidates of Dokusai-kun must not have committed any grave violations or predatory conduct.

<sup>3</sup> Dokusai-kun may declare to pass the crown onto another person. Said person is not exempt from paragraph 2's restriction.

## Art. 6 Impeachment
<sup>1</sup> Dokusai-kun is subject to impeachment.

<sup>2</sup> If 25% of the server's total active natural members are in favour of impeachment, Dokusai-kun is removed from position.
