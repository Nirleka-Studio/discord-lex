# SR 510 — The Dokusai-kun and The Server

> **Enactment Notice:** Enacted in accordance to Title 4 of the Server Charter (SR 100), by the Director

---

## Title 1: The Dokusai

#### Art. 1 Purpose
Dokusai-kun shall be the symbol and Dicktator of the server and its unity.

#### Art. 2 Authority
Dokusai-kun remains subjected under the law and the Administration.

#### Art. 3 Power
Dokusai-kun may exercise any power that the Administration does not restrict.

#### Art. 4 Restriction on Powers
<sup>1</sup> Dokusai-kun shall not give roles to members that holds administrative permissions.

<sup>2</sup> Dokusai-kun shall not give any currency to another member.

<sup>3</sup> Dokusai-kun shall not delete a channel unilaterally.

#### Art. 5 Passing the Crown
<sup>1</sup> If Dokusai-kun did not send a message or done an interaction in the last 90-days, the People may do a server-wide vote to replace Dokusai-kun with another member.

<sup>2</sup> Candidates of Dokusai-kun shall not have comitted any grave violations or predatory conduct.

<sup>3</sup> Dokusai-kun may declare to pass the crown onto another person. Said person is not exempt from paragraph 2's restriction.

#### Art. 6 Impeachment
<sup>1</sup> Dokusai-kun shall be subject to impeachment.

<sup>2</sup> If 25% of the server's total active natural members are in favour of impeachment, Dokusai-kun shall be removed from position.
