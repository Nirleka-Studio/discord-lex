# SR 515 — The Kai-Dokusai and The Server

> **Enactment Notice:** Enacted in accordance to Chapter 4 of the Server Charter (SR 100), by the Director

---

## Preamble
In the name of The Cat!

The Director of the Nirleka Studios Discord Server and his remaining sanity,

in order to stir more chaos to the pot within an otherwise stable Server,

does hereby decree:

## Chapter 1: The Kai-Dokusai

### Section 1: Principles

#### Art. 1 Subordination
The Kai-Dokusai is subordinate to the Dokusai-kun.

#### Art. 2 Under law
<sup>1</sup> The Kai-Dokusai remains subjected under the Server Charter (SR 100) and its subsequent laws.

### Section 2: Powers

#### Art. 3 General powers
<sup>1</sup> The Kai-Dokusai shall not exercise power not explicitly defined in this Act.

<sup>2</sup> The Kai-Dokusai may:\
a. change the Server name;\
b. modify channel names;\
c. create new channels; and\
d. host events.

#### Art. 4 Legislation
<sup>1</sup> The Kai-Dokusai shall have legislative power similar to the Director restricted by the bounds of the Server Charter.

<sup>2</sup> The Director holds ultimate legislative power and shall have the power to override the decisions of the Kai-Dokusai.

#### Art. 5 Executive power
<sup>1</sup> The Kai-Dokusai shall not possess executive power.

<sup>2</sup> The Kai-Dokusai however may request a member of the Executive Branch to carry out a lawful order.

## Chapter 2: The Kai-Dokusai and The Server

### Section 1: Power of the Members

#### Art. 6 Election
The Kai-Dokusai shall only be elected by members of the Server by a majority vote if no Kai-Dokusai is currently in seat.

#### Art. 7 Holding the seat
The Kai-Dokusai may remain in power for life until inactivity after 90 days, resignation, or impeachment.

#### Art. 8 Impeachment
The Kai-Dokusai may be impeached by a majority-vote (50% + 1) and removed from position.
