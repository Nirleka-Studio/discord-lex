# SR 620 — The Corner Act

> **Enactment Notice:** Enacted in accordance to Title 4 of the Server Charter (SR 100), by the Director

---

## Section 1: Definitions and Principles

### Art. 1 Definitions
In this Act:\
a. *corner* means a server channel created specifically for a member.

b. *to archive* means putting a channel in the Public Archive category to remain hidden from the public but can be accessed by those who have Archive Access.

### Art. 2 Principles
<sup>1</sup> The owner of a corner reserves the right to talk in that corner and may prohibit other members from interacting in their corner.

<sup>2</sup> Members of Administration are exempt from paragraph 1 if they have reasonable means and intentions.

<sup>3</sup> The People ultimately owns the server and may order the archival of a corner.

<sup>4</sup> A corner shall not be deleted permanently.

### Art. 3 Permanent corners
<sup>1</sup> The following are designated as permanent corners and can not be removed or taxed:\
a. `nirleka-corner`;\
b. `error-corner`; and\
c. `faze-corner`

### Art. 4 Existing corners
For existing corners, the owner of those corners shall pay for their corner tax on 19 August 2026.

## Section 2: Purchasing and Taxing Corners

### Art. 5 Purchasing corners
<sup>1</sup> A member may purchase a new corner or restore their old one for 50400 ***F R A N C S***.

<sup>2</sup> Members who have committed violations of the Codes shall not acquire any corners.

<sup>3</sup> Members who have purchased a corner shall not purchase another corner.

<sup>4</sup> Once a member have purchased a new corner, a corner with their name or nickname followed by `-corner` shall be created, and be put under the Text Channels category.

### Art. 6 Taxation of corners
<sup>1</sup> The Director shall tax all non-permanent corners for 1600 ***F R A N C S*** per week.

<sup>2</sup> The owner of a corner have 48-hours to compensate the tax once rent is due.

<sup>3</sup> If an owner fails to compensate for the tax, the corner shall be archived.
