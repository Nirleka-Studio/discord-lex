# SR 620 — The Corner Act

*The Director of Nirleka Studios,*

based on Article 33 of the Server Charter[^1],

*decrees:*

## Chapter 1: Purpose and Principles

### Section 1: Corners and Owner Rights

#### Art. 1 Purpose
This Act governs the ownership of Corners.

#### Art. 2 A Corner
<sup>1</sup> A Corner is a Server channel that is owned by a Member.

<sup>2</sup> The owner is entitled to the permissions of managing their Corner.

#### Art. 3 Naming
<sup>1</sup> By default, a Corner is named with the owner's name and ending with the suffix `-corner` by the time of its creation.

<sup>2</sup> The owner may rename it.

<sup>3</sup> The name of a Corner must be clear to a reasonable person that it is a Corner.

#### Art. 4 Permanent Corners
<sup>1</sup> The Corners defined in Annex 4 are designated as permanent corners and their owners are not required to pay rent.

<sup>2</sup> The Director may designate a Corner as permanent.

### Section 2: Permissions, Supremacy of the Law, and Administrative Measures

#### Art. 5 Permissions
The owner of a Corner has permissions specifically for their Corner as defined in Annex 1.

#### Art. 6 Reservation of law
The owner of a Corner may not undertake any action concerning their Corner not expressly authorised by law.

#### Art. 7 Administrative measures
Any lawful measure by the Administration may override the rights and privileges granted to the Owners by this Act.

### Section 3: Limits on the Number of Corners

#### Art. 8 Limits of Corners
<sup>1</sup> The limit on the number of Corners is defined in Annex 2.

<sup>2</sup> The Director or the Head Administrator may set the limit on the number of Corners.

#### Art. 9 Excessive number of Corners
<sup>1</sup> By default, when a number of Corners exceed the set limit, the newest Corners are closed.

<sup>2</sup> The Director or the Head Administrator may decide which Corners get closed when the limit is reached.

### Section 4: Closing

#### Art. 10 Closing a Corner
<sup>1</sup> A Corner is closed by the procedures defined in Annex 3.

<sup>2</sup> A Corner may not be permanently deleted.

#### Art. 11 Vacant Corner
<sup>1</sup> A Corner is considered vacant when the owner:
a. leaves the Server;
b. their account is deleted or disabled; or
c. is evicted.

<sup>2</sup> The original owner of a vacant Corner does not own the Corner any more and lose all rights of it.

<sup>3</sup> Administrators have broad discretion on whether to close a vacant Corner or not.

#### Art. 12 Eviction
<sup>1</sup> An eviction vacates an owner's Corner.

<sup>2</sup> The owner of a Corner must be evicted immediately when they are no longer in good standing.

#### Art. 13 Good standing
A person is considered in good standing if they have no history of bad faith, malicious behaviour, abuse, or corruption.

## Chapter 2: Acquiring Corners and Rent Pay

### Section 1: Purchasing Corners

#### Art. 14 Principles
<sup>1</sup> A member must be in good standing to own a Corner.

<sup>2</sup> A member may not own more than 1 Corner.

#### Art. 15 Managing authorities
The managing authorities are the Director and the Head Administrator.

#### Art. 16 Purchasing
<sup>1</sup> Purchasing procedures are outlined in Annex 5.

<sup>2</sup> Upon payment of the acquisition price:
a. if the purchaser holds a vacant or previously closed Corner, that Corner is reopened and assigned to them; or
b. otherwise, a new Corner is created.

<sup>3</sup> The rent billing cycle initiates immediately upon purchase.

#### Art. 17 Rent
<sup>1</sup> Any owner of a non-permanent Corner must pay a rent defined in Annex 5.

<sup>2</sup> If the owner fails to pay the rent for collection by the managing authorities, they are evicted.

#### Art. 18 Rent payment cycle
If the managing authorities fails to collect the rent, the owner may keep their Corner and the billing cycle may continue and accumulate the final rent cost after each complete cycle.

## Annex 1
(Art. 5)

The permissions given to the Owner of a Corner are:
1. Manage Threads;
2. Bypass Slowdown;
3. Pin Messages.

## Annex 2
(Art. 8)

The limit of Corners is 6.

## Annex 3
(Art. 10)

A Corner is closed by archiving it in the Public Archive category to remain hidden from the public but can be accessed by those who have Archive Access.

## Annex 4
(Art. 4)

The following Corners are permanent:
1. `nirleka-corner`;
2. `error-corner`;
3. `faze-corner`;
4. `ueef-corner`.

## Annex 5
(Art. 16, 17)

### 1. Acquisition
<sup>1</sup> The purchase price of a Corner is 50,400₣, payable directly from the buyer's balance by a managing authority upon acquisition.

### 2. Rent and Payment Terms
<sup>1</sup> The recurring rent for a non-permanent Corner is 1,600₣ per bi-weekly term (every 14 days).

<sup>2</sup> Rent must be paid by the owner within the 14-day billing cycle, subject to a 48-hour grace period before eviction proceedings initiate.
