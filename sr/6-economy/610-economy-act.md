# SR 610 — The Economy Act

> **Enactment Notice:** Enacted in accordance with Chapter 4 of the Server Charter (SR 101), by the Director

---

## Section 1: Currency and Issuing Authority

### Art. 1 Currency unit
<sup>1</sup> The currency of the Server is the Nirleka franc.

<sup>2</sup> The Nirleka franc may be denoted by `₣` or ***F R A N C S***.

### Art. 2 Issuing authority
<sup>1</sup> The Director may pay a sum of money to any member at the Director’s discretion.

<sup>2</sup> The Head Administrator and the Director each have the authority to impose a financial penalty on, or recover a sum from, a member who commits a violation in accordance with the law.

## Section 2: Income, Transactions, and Fines

### Art. 3 Income
<sup>1</sup> Members may receive a variable reward of not less than 5₣ and not more than 15₣ per message sent in a public channel, subject to a minimum interval restriction of 1 minute between qualifying rewards.

<sup>2</sup> Members serving in the Administration may receive an allowance of a fixed amount.

<sup>3</sup> Members serving in the Administration must not receive any financial remuneration or reward for moderation actions.

### Art. 4 Transactions
<sup>1</sup> Transactions of Nirleka francs in any way must only be restricted within the features of the server.

<sup>2</sup> Violating paragraph 1 incurs a 16000₣ fine and 48-hour timeout.

### Art. 6 Fines
The Administration may codify a fixed fine for violations of law.
