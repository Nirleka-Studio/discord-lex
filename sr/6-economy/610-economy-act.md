# SR 610 — The Economy Act

> **Enactment Notice:** Enacted in accordance to Title 4 of the Server Charter (SR 100), by the Director

---

## Section 1: Currency and Issuing Authority

### Art. 1 Currency unit
The currency of the Nirleka Studios Discord server shall be the Nirleka franc. Denoted by `₣` or ***F R A N C S***.

### Art. 2 Issuing authority
<sup>1</sup> The power to manually give money to another member shall be the Director.

<sup>2</sup> The power to remove money from another member for violations of the Codes shall be the Head Administrator and Director.

## Section 2: Income, Transactions, and Fines

### Art. 3 Limitations
Art. 9 of the Administration Codes (SR 410) governs all use of ***F R A N C S*** in the Administration.

### Art. 4 Income
<sup>1</sup> Members shall receive a variable reward of not less than 5 ***F R A N C S*** and not more than 15 ***F R A N C S*** per message sent in a public channel, subject to a minimum interval restriction of one (1) minute between qualifying rewards.

<sup>2</sup> Members serving in the Administration may receive a fixed amount of ***F R A N C S***.

<sup>3</sup> Members serving in the Administration shall not receive ***F R A N C S*** as a reward for moderation actions.

### Art. 5 Transactions
<sup>1</sup> Transactions using the ***F R A N C S*** in any way shall only be restricted within the features of the server.

<sup>2</sup> Violating paragraph 1 shall result in a 16000 ***F R A N C S*** fine and 48-hour timeout.

### Art. 6 Fines
The Administration may codify a fixed fine for violations of the Codes.
