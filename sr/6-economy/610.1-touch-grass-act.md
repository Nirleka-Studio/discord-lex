# SR 610.1 — The Touch Grass Act

> **Enactment Notice:** Enacted in accordance to Title 4 of the Server Charter (SR 100), by the Director

---

## Section 1: General Provisions

### Art. 1 Definitions
<sup>1</sup> In this Act:\
a. *touch grass* means a natural person being present outdoors, at least roughly 500 meters away from one's home(s).

b. *proof* means any way to prove that one has touched grass. This includes but not limited to, photos, livestreams, or GPS tracking.

## Section 2: Specific Provisions

### Art. 2 Payout for Touching Grass
<sup>1</sup> Members shall be compensated for providing proof of touching grass.

<sup>2</sup> Proofs dated before August 2026 are invalid.

<sup>3</sup> Members may receive a minimum of 250 to 660 ***F R A N C S*** for proof(s) per 1-hour period.

<sup>4</sup> Members shall not submit a proof that was not taken by them or another individual that was with them.

<sup>5</sup> Members submitting a fake proof shall receive:
a. a warning on the first offence;
b. a 1-week timeout and a fine of 1200 ***F R A N C S***.
