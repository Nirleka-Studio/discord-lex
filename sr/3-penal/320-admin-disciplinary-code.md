# SR 320 – Administration Disciplinary Code
*(ADC, AdminDisCo)

*The Director of Nirleka Studios,*

based on Article 2 and 24 of the Server Charter (SR 101),

decrees:

## Chapter 1: General Provisions

### Section 1: Fundamentals

#### Art. 1 No penalty without a law
A penalty or measure may only be imposed for an act that has been expressly declared to be an offence by law.

#### Art. 2 Scope
<sup>1</sup> This Code applies to any offence committed by any Administrator on or after the date it comes into force.

<sup>2</sup> For offences committed before the date this Code comes into force, the regulations and penalties in effect at the time of the offence apply.

#### Art. 3 Prohibition of double jeopardy
No person who has been convicted or acquitted in the Server by a final legally binding judgment may be prosecuted again for the same offence.

#### Art. 4 An Administrator
An Administrator is a member of Administration, including the positions defined in Chapter 2 of the Server Charter (SR 101), and their appointed staff.

#### Art. 5 Equality
The persons subject to penal laws for the Administration remains subject to ordinary penal laws and other Server laws.

### Section 2: Intent and Negligence

#### Art. 6 Terms
<sup>1</sup> Unless the law expressly provides otherwise, an offence is punishable only if it is committed intentionally.

<sup>2</sup> A person commits an offence intentionally if they carry out the act with knowledge and intent. It is also intentional if the perpetrator considers the commission of the offence to be possible and accepts its occurrence.

<sup>3</sup> A person commits an offence negligently if they fail to consider or take into account the consequences of their conduct due to a breach of their duty of care. Negligence is inadvertent if the offender fails to exercise the caution to which they are obliged under the circumstances and according to their personal situation.

#### Art. 7 Mistake of fact
<sup>1</sup> If the offender acts under a mistaken appreciation of the facts, judicial authority must judge the act according to the facts as the offender believed them to be.

<sup>2</sup> If the offender could have avoided the mistake had they exercised due care, they are punishable for negligence if the negligent commission of the act is an offence.

#### Art. 8 Acts authorised by law
A person acts lawfully if their conduct is required or authorised by law, even if that conduct would otherwise constitute an offence under this Code or any other law.

### Section 3: Participation

#### Art. 9 Incitement and complicity
<sup>1</sup> A person who intentionally induces another person to commit an offence (inciter) is liable to the same penalty as the principal perpetrator.

<sup>2</sup> A person who intentionally assists another person in committing an offence (accomplice) is subject to a mitigated penalty.

#### Art. 10 Attempt
<sup>1</sup> An attempt occurs when a person initiates the execution of an offence but fails to complete it.

<sup>2</sup> An attempt is punishable only if the offence itself is punishable when committed intentionally. Judicial authority may mitigate the penalty for an attempted offence.

<sup>3</sup> A person who voluntarily abandons the execution of an offence or prevents its completion is punished for the attempt.

### Section 4: Terminology

#### Art. 11 Moderation action
<sup>1</sup> A moderation action is any exercise of administrative or technical power that restricts, alters, removes, or penalizes a member’s ability to participate, communicate, or access features within the Server.

<sup>2</sup> Moderation actions include, in particular:
a. timeouts, mutes, and suspensions;
b. the deletion, modification, or hiding of messages or content;
c. the revocation of permissions or roles; and
d. the imposition of fines, kicks, or bans.

## Chapter 2: Penalties and Measures

### Art. 12 Timeout
<sup>1</sup> A timeout deprives an Administrator of the ability to:
a. interact with other members on the Server;
b. alter any Server features or configurations; and
c. exercise any power or technical privilege attached to their position.

<sup>2</sup> A timeout must be imposed for a minimum duration of 1 hour and a maximum duration of 28 days.

<sup>3</sup> The native Discord timeout feature may be used to execute this measure.

### Art. 13 Suspension
<sup>1</sup> A suspension deprives an Administrator of the right to exercise any administrative, technical, or judicial power derived from their position for a specified period not exceeding 90 days.

<sup>2</sup> During a suspension, the Administrator retains their position but loses access to administrative channels, logs, and permission sets.

### Art. 14 Removal from position
<sup>1</sup> Removal from position consists of the permanent revocation of the Administrator’s specific role, title, and assigned duties within the Administration.

<sup>2</sup> A person removed from position under this Article may not be reappointed to an administrative role for a period of at least 4 months.

### Art. 15 Demotion
A demotion consists of stripping the Administrator of their current tier or rank and assigning them to a lower subordinate role within the Administration hierarchy.

### Art. 16 Fine
<sup>1</sup> A fine is a financial penalty imposing a fixed liability in Nirleka francs (₣) on the convicted Administrator, in accordance with Article 2, Paragraph 2 and Article 6 of the Economy Act (SR 610).

<sup>2</sup> The execution of a fine takes place through the immediate and automatic deduction of the specified amount from the Administrator’s account balance by the competent authority. If the account balance is insufficient to satisfy the full amount, the remaining sum constitutes a binding debt against the member, resulting in a negative balance.

<sup>3</sup> Unless an article in Chapter 3 explicitly provides for a fixed fine, a fine is imposed:
a. as a standalone penalty of not less than 100₣ and not more than 5,000₣; or
b. as a supplementary penalty alongside a timeout, suspension, or demotion.

<sup>4</sup> The authority determining the sentence assesses the amount of the fine taking into account:
a. the severity of the offence; and
b. the financial situation of the offender at the time of judgment.

<sup>5</sup> If the debt remains outstanding for more than 14 days following a final judgment, the authority may convert the unpaid balance into a suspension at a rate of 1 day of suspension for every 100₣ outstanding, up to a maximum of 30 days.

## Chapter 3: Offences

### Section 1: Offences Against Administrative Authority and Duties

#### Art. 17 Neglect of duty
An Administrator who, through gross negligence, fails to perform an administrative duty assigned to them by law or order, causing disruption to Server operations or administration, is punished with a timeout not exceeding 7 days, a fine not exceeding 1,000₣, or both.

#### Art. 18 Disobeying a lawful order
An Administrator who intentionally refuses or fails to execute a lawful directive or order issued by a superior administrative authority is punished with a timeout not exceeding 14 days, a fine not exceeding 2,500₣, or suspension.

#### Art. 19 Execution of an unlawful order
<sup>1</sup> An Administrator who executes an order knowing, or under circumstances where they ought to have known, that the order violates Server law is punished with a timeout not exceeding 7 days or a fine not exceeding 1,000₣.

<sup>2</sup> If the Administrator acted under direct threat of retaliatory administrative action by a superior, the penalty may be mitigated or waived.

#### Art. 20 Excess of official authority
An Administrator who exercises an administrative, technical, or judicial power not granted to them by law, or who exercises a power in direct violation of express statutory restrictions, is punished with a suspension, a fine not exceeding 5,000₣, or removal from position.

### Section 2: Offences Against Administrative Records and Integrity

#### Art. 21 Failure to log moderation actions
An Administrator who carries out a moderation action without recording the required entry in the official audit log or designated record within the prescribed timeframe is punished with a fine not exceeding 500₣.

#### Art. 22 Tampering with moderation logs
An Administrator who intentionally alters, falsifies, deletes, or conceals an official audit log, moderation record, or administrative entry is punished with a suspension, a fine not exceeding 5,000₣, or removal from position.

#### Art. 23 Bribery
<sup>1</sup> An Administrator who solicits, accepts, or promises to accept Nirleka francs, real-world currency, or any other personal advantage in exchange for performing or omitting an official administrative act is punished with removal from position and a fine of not less than 2,000₣.

<sup>2</sup> Whoever offers, promises, or grants an advantage to an Administrator to influence their official conduct is subject to penalties under ordinary Server law.

#### Art. 24 Abuse of power
An Administrator who intentionally uses their administrative position, access privileges, or technical powers to secure an unlawful advantage for themselves or a third party, or to intentionally cause harm to a member, is punished with a suspension, a fine not exceeding 5,000₣, or removal from position.

### Section 3: Offences Against Member Rights and Public Order

#### Art. 25 Unlawful moderation
An Administrator who intentionally imposes a ban, kick, timeout, or other restriction on a member without a factual basis or legal ground provided by Server law is punished with a timeout not exceeding 14 days, a fine not exceeding 2,500₣, or suspension.

#### Art. 26 Targeted harassment and personal hostility
An Administrator who uses their official position or public communication channels to repeatedly insult, intimidate, dox, or publish private personal details of a member or another Administrator without consent is punished with a suspension, a fine not exceeding 5,000₣, or removal from position.

#### Art. 27 Instigation of public disorder
An Administrator who publicly incites members to violate Server law, disobey administrative orders, or disrupt official Server functions is punished with a suspension or removal from position.
