# SR 321 — Martial Disciplinary Procedure Code

*The Director of Nirleka Studios,*

based on Article 32 of the Server Charter[^1],

*decrees:*

## Title 1: Organisation

### Chapter 1: Judicial Organisation

#### Section 1: Principles

##### Art. 1 Independence
The judicial independence of formal martial justice is guaranteed.

##### Art. 2 Principle of expeditiousness
Martial justice must be executed immediately and concluded without unjustified delay.

##### Art. 3 Martial justice
Martial justice concerns justice of the Administration.

#### Section 2: Judicial authorities

##### Art. 4 The Judge
<sup>1</sup> The Judge in a formal proceeding is the Secretary.

<sup>2</sup> Where the Secretary is unavailable, the Director may act as judge or appoint one.

##### Art. 5 Abstention required
Judicial authorities determining a case must abstain if they:
a. have a personal interest in the cause;
b. are related to a party by blood or marriage, in a direct line or in a collateral line-up to the third degree; or
c. have acted previously in the same matter as a witness, helper, or private advisor to either side.

##### Art. 6 Principle of necessity
Where all judicial authorities have a conflict of interest in the case, the Director may act as Judge or appoint one.

### Chapter 2: Enforcing Authority

#### Art. 7 The enforcing authorities
The enforcing authorities are:
a. members of the Administration's Executive Branch as defined in Chapter 2, Section 1 of the Server Charter[^1]; and
b. their appointed staff.

## Title 2: Enforcement

### Chapter 1: General

#### Art. 8 Punishing violations of law
<sup>1</sup> Unless stated otherwise in Title 3, upon an Administrator violating a law, an enforcing authority must immediately:
a. impose a measure outlined by law; and
b. document relevant administrative and moderation actions.

<sup>2</sup> Where an enforcing authority holds a lower rank than the offender, they must report to a higher available enforcing authority.

<sup>3</sup> The enforcing authorities must inform the offender on what law and specific, relevant provisions the offender has been charged, acquitted, and punished for.

#### Art. 9 Documentation
<sup>1</sup> The Director or Head Administrator outlines the specifications on what is documented when punishing an Administrator.

<sup>2</sup> They may issue an ordinance on such matter.

### Chapter 2: Notices

#### Art. 10 Notice
<sup>1</sup> A notice of punishment must clearly state:
a. the offender;
b. the exact provision of a law violated; and
c. the punishment.

<sup>2</sup> Including the offender in the notice may be simply established by mentioning the offender if present in the Server or channel.

#### Art. 11 Execution of notices
<sup>1</sup> A notice of punishment is sent in either:
a. the channel where the violation occured;
b. any general-purpose channel if the violation occured in multiple channels; or
c. the DM if the offender is off the Server.

<sup>2</sup> A notice of punishment may be sent by:
a. the punishing enforcing authority;
b. any other enforcing authority if requested or the punishing enforcing authority fails or stalls to do so; or
c. an automated system such as bots.

<sup>3</sup> A notice of punishment is written and sent immediately after the punishment is given.

### Chapter 3: Punishments and Measures

#### Art. 12 Timeout
<sup>1</sup> A timeout deprives an Administrator of the ability to:
a. interact with other members on the Server;
b. alter any Server features or configurations; and
c. exercise any power or technical privilege attached to their position.

<sup>2</sup> The native Discord timeout feature may be used to execute this measure.

#### Art. 13 Suspension
<sup>1</sup> A suspension deprives an Administrator of the right to exercise any administrative, technical, or judicial power derived from their position for a specified period not exceeding 90 days.

<sup>2</sup> During a suspension, the Administrator retains their position but loses access to administrative channels, logs, and permission sets.

#### Art. 14 Removal from position
Removal from position consists of the permanent revocation of the Administrator’s specific role, title, and assigned duties within the Administration.

#### Art. 15 Demotion
A demotion consists of stripping the Administrator of their current tier or rank and assigning them to a lower subordinate role within the Administration hierarchy.

#### Art. 16 Fine
<sup>1</sup> A fine is a financial penalty imposing a fixed liability in Nirleka francs (₣) on the convicted Administrator, in accordance with Article 2, Paragraph 2 and Article 6 of the Economy Act[^2].

<sup>2</sup> The execution of a fine takes place through the immediate and automatic deduction of the specified amount from the Administrator’s account balance by the competent authority. If the account balance is insufficient to satisfy the full amount, the remaining sum constitutes a binding debt against the member, resulting in a negative balance.

<sup>3</sup> If the debt remains outstanding for more than 14 days following a final judgment, the authority may convert the unpaid balance into a suspension at a rate of 1 day of suspension for every 100₣ outstanding, up to a maximum of 30 days.

## Title 3: Appeals and Court-Martial

### Chapter 1: Summary Punishments

#### Art. 17 Punishing infractions
For light infractions, Article 8 applies.

### Chapter 2: Court-Martial

#### Art. 18 Summary court-martial
<sup>1</sup> The Director, the Head Administrator, or a Senior Administrator, may start a court-martial against the offender repeated infractions or greater offences.

<sup>2</sup> The Judge is the highest ranking Administrator that opens the case, once a Judge is appointed no other Administrator may take over.

<sup>3</sup> The prosecuting authority has the burden of proof.

<sup>4</sup> The offender has the right to be heard for the evidence and charges brought against them.

<sup>5</sup> The Judge, acting on presented evidence, decides on whether to:
a. convict the offender guilty on all or some charges; or
b. find the offender not guilty and void the charges.
