# SR 0.1 — Extraterritorial Matters Code

> **Enactment Notice:** Enacted in accordance to Chapter 4 of the Server Charter (SR 100). Applies to all members of the Server and members of Administration.

---

## Chapter 1: General Provisions

### Section 1: Purpose and Definitions

#### Art. 1 Purpose
<sup>1</sup> This Code's purpose is described in the following:\
a. To outline cross-server and real-life matters;

b. Restrict Administrators' powers to punish users for a violation they committed outside the server; and

c. To give power to Administrators to keep members of the Server safe from bad actors.

#### Art. 2 Definitions
In this Code:

a. *off-Server* means anything off-Server. This may include other Discord servers, games, platforms, and real-life.

### Section 2: Principles

#### Art. 3 Administrative principles
<sup>1</sup> Administration is under no obligation to investigate off-Server conduct absent a report from a member or credible external source.

<sup>2</sup> The Administration shall maintain a balance between fairness, members' safety, and Administrators' sanity.

#### Art. 4 Punishing actions committed off-Server 
<sup>1</sup> The power to punish members for what they committed off-Server shall be restricted under this Code and laws under SR Category 0.

<sup>2</sup> In accordance to Art. 1. (Principle of Legality) of the Server Charter (SR 100), the Server shall make all members aware that they can be punished for some actions they committed off-Server.

#### Art. 5 Eligibility for punishment
<sup>1</sup> As a principle, a member shall only be punished for actions off-Server to protect the safety of the Server.

<sup>2</sup> Punishable actions includes, but are not limited to:

a. predatory conduct;
b. doxxing another user;
c. targeted harassment, blackmail, or credible threats of harm; and
d. malicious distribution of malware, phishing links, scams, and other illegal materials.

#### Art. 6 Administrative discretion
The Head Administrator and Director reserves the sole right to assess severity and determine appropriate administrative action.

#### Art. 7 Burden of proof
In accordance to Art. 3 (Presumption of Innocence) of the Server Chapter, substantial and verifiable proof is required to punish a member.

#### Art. 8 Suspension
<sup>1</sup> A suspension is an act of temporarily pausing all Server DMs and banning the alleged member from the Server.

<sup>2</sup> No messages from the alleged member shall be removed when banning.

<sup>3</sup> If substantial evidence for an allegation is presented, Administrators can suspend the alleged member immediately, and Art. 8 must follows.

#### Art. 9 Right to due process
<sup>1</sup> In accordance to Art. 2 (Due process) of the Server Charter, alleged members have the right to due process.

<sup>2</sup> Before suspension, Administrators shall send a DM message to the alleged member on the situation.

<sup>3</sup> During suspension, an Administrator shall remain reasonably available to receive a defence from the alleged member during the suspension period.

<sup>4</sup> If the alleged member defended with substantial counter-evidence, suspension shall be lifted.

<sup>5</sup> If the alleged member outright refuses or did not respond to defend themselves, Server DMs can be opened after 24-hours while the alleged member maintains their suspension until paragraph 4 is met.

## Chapter 2: Specific Provisions

### Section 1: Problematic platforms

#### Art. 10 Problematic platforms
A problematic platform is any Discord server or other community platforms that is largely and openly infested with bad actors and / or the platform's administration and / or owner are bad actors themselves.

#### Art. 11 Members in problematic platforms
<sup>1</sup> A member of the Server prohibited to be in any problematic platforms.

<sup>2</sup> A member of the Server that is in or associated with problematic platforms shall be suspended immediately.

<sup>3</sup> Victims, lurkers, or researchers, members who stay in a malicious server purely to monitor safety threats against themselves or their friends are exempt from paragraph 1 and 2.

<sup>4</sup> Victims shall be asked to leave the servers immediately. Failure to comply shall result in a suspension.

### Section 2: Registered sex offenders

#### Art. 12 Accounts
<sup>1</sup> A member of the Server whose account is operated by a confirmed registered sex offender shall be suspended immediately.

<sup>2</sup> A member shall only be confirmed via a public sex offender registry or equivalent official record.
