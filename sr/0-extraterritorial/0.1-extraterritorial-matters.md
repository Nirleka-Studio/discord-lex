# SR 0.1 — Extraterritorial Matters Code

> **Enactment Notice:** Enacted in accordance with Chapter 4 of the Server Charter (SR 100). Applies to all members of the Server and members of Administration.

---

## Chapter 1: General Provisions

### Section 1: Purpose and Definitions

#### Art. 1 Purpose
<sup>1</sup> This Code aims to:

a. outline cross-server and real-life matters;

b. restrict Administrators' power to punish members for violations committed outside the Server; and

c. empower Administrators to protect Server members from bad actors.

#### Art. 2 Definitions
In this Code:

a. *off-Server* means any space outside the Server, including other Discord servers, games, platforms, and real life.

### Section 2: Principles

#### Art. 3 Administrative principles
<sup>1</sup> Administration is not required to investigate off-server conduct unless a member or credible external source reports it.

<sup>2</sup> Administration must balance fairness, member safety, and Administrator workload.

#### Art. 4 Punishing actions committed off-Server
<sup>1</sup> This Code and SR Category 0 laws restrict the power of Administrators to punish members for off-Server conduct.

<sup>2</sup> The Server must notify all members that Administrators may punish them for certain off-Server conduct.

#### Art. 5 Eligibility for punishment
<sup>1</sup> Administrators may only punish a member for off-Server actions to protect Server safety.

<sup>2</sup> Punishable actions include, but are not limited to:

a. predatory conduct;
b. doxxing another user;
c. targeted harassment, blackmail, or credible threats of harm; and
d. malicious distribution of malware, phishing links, scams, or other illegal materials.

#### Art. 6 Administrative discretion
The Head Administrator and Director reserve the sole right to assess severity and determine appropriate administrative action.

#### Art. 7 Burden of proof
Administrators require substantial and verifiable proof to punish a member.

#### Art. 8 Suspension
<sup>1</sup> Suspension temporarily pauses all Server DMs and bans the alleged member from the Server.

<sup>2</sup> Administrators must not remove any messages from the alleged member when issuing a ban.

<sup>3</sup> If a reporter presents substantial evidence for an allegation, Administrators may suspend the alleged member immediately and must follow the procedure in Art. 9.

#### Art. 9 Right to due process
<sup>1</sup> Alleged members have the right to due process.

<sup>2</sup> Before issuing a suspension, Administrators must send a DM to the alleged member explaining the situation.

<sup>3</sup> During the suspension period, an Administrator must remain reasonably available to receive a defence from the alleged member.

<sup>4</sup> If the alleged member provides substantial counter-evidence, Administrators must lift the suspension.

<sup>5</sup> If the alleged member refuses or fails to respond, Administrators may open Server DMs after 24 hours while maintaining the member's suspension until the member satisfies paragraph 4.

## Chapter 2: Specific Provisions

### Section 1: Problematic platforms

#### Art. 10 Problematic platforms
A problematic platform is any Discord server or community platform where bad actors openly infest the community, or where the platform's administration or owner are bad actors themselves.

#### Art. 11 Members in problematic platforms
<sup>1</sup> Members of the Server must not join or participate in any problematic platform.

<sup>2</sup> Administrators must immediately suspend any Server member who joins or remains associated with a problematic platform.

<sup>3</sup> Paragraphs 1 and 2 do not apply to victims, lurkers, or researchers who stay in a problematic server solely to monitor safety threats against themselves or others.

<sup>4</sup> Administrators must instruct victims to leave problematic servers immediately. If a victim fails to comply, Administrators must issue a suspension.

### Section 2: Registered sex offenders

#### Art. 12 Accounts
<sup>1</sup> Administrators must immediately suspend any member whose account a confirmed registered sex offender operates.

<sup>2</sup> Administrators may only confirm a member's status as a registered sex offender through a public sex offender registry or equivalent official record.
