# SR 415 — Courts and Appeals Proceedings Code

> **Enactment Notice:** Enacted in accordance to Chapter 4 of the Server Charter (SR 100). Applies to all members of Administration.

---

## Section 1: Definitions and Principles

### Art. 1: Definitions
In this Code:\
a. *Administrator* means a member of Administration or one of their appointed staff.

b. *defendant* means a natural member being accused of a violation of the Codes or other laws.

c. *ticket* means a designated chat for a particular case.

### Art. 2: Principles
<sup>1</sup> A defendant must be aware of what they are charged and punished for.

<sup>2</sup> No person of judicial authority shall determine a case that they are personally involved in.

## Section 2: Roles

### Art. 3: Judge
<sup>1</sup> The Judge shall be responsible for the final decision if the defendant is guilty and what punishment is legally applicable.

<sup>2</sup> The Judge shall not lower the punishment as a result of harassment or pressure from the defendant or their supporters.

<sup>3</sup> The Secretary shall assume the role of Judge, if they are available within 24-hours. If not, the Head Administrator or Director may assume the role if they are not personally involved in the case.

<sup>4</sup> The Judge may hold someone in contempt of court for disrespecting or derailing court order, and can give a penalty of:
a. a timeout with a maximum duration of 24 hours; and
b. a fine of a maximum of 5000₣; and

### Art. 4: Prosecutor
<sup>1</sup> The Prosecutor shall be responsible for determining Code and law violations and recommend a penalty.

<sup>2</sup> The Head Administrator or Director shall assume the role of Prosecutor if they are not personally involved in the case.

## Section 3: Proceedings

### Art. 5 Initial filing
<sup>1</sup> A person may be reported for violations and a Prosecutor shall file the case and open a ticket within 24-hours.

<sup>2</sup> Violations of the Penal Code by a regular member does not require a case.

<sup>3</sup> The accused person becomes the Defendant in their case.

### Art. 6 Suspension
<sup>1</sup> Suspension is the act of removing an Administrator of administrative powers.

<sup>2</sup> A suspended Administrator shall be stripped of their roles and permissions and shall not affect any decisions of Administration.

<sup>3</sup> An Administrator shall be suspended once substantial evidence of violations are presented.

### Art. 7 Proceedings
<sup>1</sup> The Prosecutor shall look for and present evidence and determine what violation of law the Defendant has committed.

<sup>2</sup> The Defendant may counter their charges with evidence or assertions. The Prosecutor shall look for and present evidence to determine if the assertions are true.

<sup>3</sup> Proceedings shall take place in the case ticket and no other places.

### Art. 8 Ruling
<sup>1</sup> If the Defendant or Prosecutor does not have any more evidence or argument to present, the Judge shall determine their ruling.

<sup>2</sup> The Judge present their ruling in the ticket.

<sup>3</sup> After the Judge present their ruling, their ruling shall be final.

<sup>4</sup> If the Defendant is found guilty they shall be punished as described by the Judge.

## Section 4: Appeals

### Art. 9 Appeals
<sup>1</sup> If a member somehow believes that they are not guilty after their ruling and punishment, they may file an appeal to a Court of Appeals.

<sup>2</sup> To prevent abuse, a member may file an appeal for free, provided they have new evidence. If a member submits an appeal in bad faith or lack of evidence, they may be fined for 1500₣.

<sup>3</sup> Should a member want to file an appeal again after an initial fine, they shall require 2000₣ to do so.

<sup>4</sup> Payment to the Court of Appeals shall be evenly distributed to all workers who handled the case.

<sup>5</sup> Members of a Court of Appeals shall only decline an appeal due to the lack of new evidence. Violation of this paragraph shall result in removal from position.

## Section 5 Court of Appeals

### Art. 10 Court of Appeals
<sup>1</sup> The Secretary shall have to power to lawfully appoint, remove, and oversee members of the Court of Appeals.

<sup>2</sup> The Director may assume the role of a Court of Appeals if no worker of the Court is available.

## Section 6 Miscellaneous

### Art. 11 Clerical errors
<sup>1</sup> If a Prosecutor somehow cited the wrong article, code, or code version with no bad faith, the case shall be reopened for a corrected ruling under the correct citations.

<sup>2</sup> Miscitation under bad faith is punishable by:
a. a warning on the first offence; and
b. a 1-week timeout and removal from position on the second offence.
