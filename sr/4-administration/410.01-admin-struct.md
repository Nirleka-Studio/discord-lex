# SR 410.01 — Temporary Ordinance on the Positions of the Administration
*(TOPA)*

*The Director of Nirleka Studios,*

based on Article 33 of the Server Charter[^1],

*decrees:*

## Chapter 1: General Provisions

### Art. 1 Purpose
The purpose of this Ordinance is to define the structure and powers of the positions of the Administration, mainly the Executive Branch.

### Art. 2 General powers
<sup>1</sup> All Administrators may:
a. view the Server Audit Log; and
b. view the channels under the Administration category.

<sup>2</sup> Executive Administrators may, excluding the Chief Minister:
a. delete messages; and
b. manage the roles of members;

## Chapter 2: Specific Provisions

### Section 1: Lance Administrators

#### Art. 3 Powers of Lance Administrators
A Lance Administrator may:
a. timeout;
b. kick; and
c. ban.

#### Art. 4 Appointment of Lance Administrators
The Director or Head Administrator may appoint and remove Lance Administrators.

### Section 2: Senior Administrators

#### Art. 5 Powers of Senior Administrators
<sup>1</sup> A Senior Administrator has the powers of the Lance Administrators.

<sup>2</sup> A Senior Administrator may override administrative decisions and measures of lower ranks as the law allows.

#### Art. 6 Appointment of Senior Administrators
The Director or Head Administrator may appoint and remove Senior Administrators.

### Section 3: The Head Administrator

#### Art. 7 Powers of the Head Administrator
<sup>1</sup> The Head Administrator has all the powers Lance and Senior Administrators have.

<sup>2</sup> The Head Administrator may:
a. manage features of the Server as their duties require them.
