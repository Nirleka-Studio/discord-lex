# SR 410 — Administration Codes Compilations
*(Administration Codes)*

> **Enactment Notice:** Enacted in accordance with Chapter 4 of the Server Charter (SR 100). Applies to all members of Administration.

---

## Chapter 1: Purpose and Scope

#### Art. 1 Purpose
The Administration Codes and subsequent laws govern the conduct of the Administration and its procedures.

#### Art. 2 Scope
The Administration Codes and subsequent laws apply to all members of Administration, including all Administrators holding positions defined in Chapter 2 of the Server Charter (SR 100) and their appointed staff.

## Chapter 2: General Provisions

### Section 1: Definitions and Principles

#### Art. 3 Definitions
<sup>1</sup> In this Code:

a. *Administrator* means any member of Administration or their appointed staff.

b. *moderation action* means any action taken to enforce rules or manage members, including warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, message deletions, and thread closures.

#### Art. 4 Principles
<sup>1</sup> Administrators must enforce all laws equally among all members of the Server.

<sup>2</sup> Administrators must maintain a peaceful, safe, and orderly environment for the Server while balancing individual liberty.

<sup>3</sup> During normal operations, Administrators are encouraged to maintain close relationships with members of the Server.

<sup>4</sup> During conflicts, Administrators must maintain professionalism and emotional distance.

<sup>5</sup> For any loopholes, flaws, or situations not specified in law or procedure, Administrators must act in good faith to uphold paragraph 1.

<sup>6</sup> Administrators must maintain full knowledge of the law and specified procedures.

<sup>7</sup> A judging authority must increase penalties for violations committed by senior Administrators.

### Section 2: Server Features

#### Art. 5 Server settings
Only the Director has the authority to modify Server settings.

#### Art. 5a Bots
Only the Head Administrator and the Director have the authority to add, remove, or modify Server bots.

---

## Chapter 3: Specific Provisions and Compilations

### Section 1: Behaviours

#### Art. 6 Professional conduct
<sup>1</sup> Administrators must treat all members with respect.

<sup>2</sup> Art. 9 of the Public Code (SR 210) limits freedom of speech.

<sup>3</sup> Constant use of slurs, harassment, or tantrums results in:

a. a warning on the first offence;

b. a 48-hour timeout on the second offence; and

c. removal from position on the third offence.

#### Art. 7 Abuse of power
<sup>1</sup> Administrators must not exercise their powers for personal gain, irritation, pettiness, or spite.

<sup>2</sup> A violation of paragraph 1 results in:

a. a 48-hour timeout on the first offence; and

b. removal from position on the second offence.

#### Art. 8 Negligence
<sup>1</sup> An Administrator must take action if available and aware that a violation of law is occurring.

<sup>2</sup> Failure to comply with paragraph 1 results in:

a. a warning on the first offence;

b. a 48-hour timeout on the second offence; and

c. removal from position on the third offence.

#### Art. 9 Bribery
Any Administrator who willingly accepts compensation from another individual in exchange for administrative influence must be removed from position and permanently barred from holding any position in Administration.

---

### Section 2: Exercise of Power and Documentation

#### Art. 10 Moderation actions
<sup>1</sup> All moderation actions and administrative acts must comply with the Penal Code and applicable laws.

<sup>2</sup> A violation of paragraph 1 results in:

a. a warning on the first offence;

b. a 48-hour timeout on the second offence; and

c. removal from position on the third offence.

<sup>3</sup> A senior or superior Administrator must immediately reverse unlawful moderation decisions and actions.

<sup>4</sup> Actions taken in bad faith by Administrators that enabling laws do not explicitly provide for remain subject to paragraph 1.

#### Art. 11 Documentation of moderation actions
<sup>1</sup> Administrators must document all moderation actions according to the procedures outlined by the Head Administrator or Director.

<sup>2</sup> Failure to log a moderation action results in:

a. a warning on the first offence;

b. a 48-hour timeout on the second offence; and

c. removal from position on the third offence.

#### Art. 12 Tampering with moderation logs
<sup>1</sup> An Administrator must not tamper with, deceptively modify, or delete moderation logs. Violations result in:

a. a 48-hour timeout on the first offence; and

b. removal from position on the second offence.

<sup>2</sup> Log modifications resulting from clerical errors are exempt from paragraph 1.

#### Art. 13 Compromised accounts
<sup>1</sup> A user account is considered compromised if it performs any of the following:

a. sends unauthorised, automated, or deceptive communications, including phishing URLs, fraudulent promotional offers, or mass user mentions;

b. alters profile metadata, display names, or profile pictures to impersonate platform support personnel, Server staff, or system bots; or

c. initiates unauthorised administrative modifications, including creating rogue webhooks, escalating role privileges, or generating bulk invites.

<sup>2</sup> Upon encountering a user exhibiting behaviours specified in paragraph 1, an Administrator must immediately ban the user.
