# SR 410 — Administration Codes Compilations
*(Administration Codes)*

> **Enactment Notice:** Enacted in accordance to Title 4 of the Server Charter (SR 100). Applies to all members of Administration.

---

## Title 1: Purpose and Scope

#### Art. 1 Purpose
The Administration Codes and its subsequent laws shall govern the conduct of the Administration and its procedures.

#### Art. 2 Scope
The Administration Codes and its subsequent laws shall apply to all members of Administration, this includes but not limited to all Administrators who holds positions defined in Title 2 of the Server Charter (SR 100) and their appointed staff.

## Title 2: General Provisions

### Section 1: Definitions and Principles

#### Art. 3 Definitions
<sup>1</sup> In this Code:\
a. *Administrators* means any member of Administration and their appointed staff.

b. *moderation action* means any action taken to enforce rules or handle members, such as warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, message deletions, thread closing, etc.

#### Art. 4 Principles
<sup>1</sup> Administrators shall enforce all laws equally to all members of the server.

<sup>2</sup> Administrators shall maintain a peaceful, safe, and orderly environment for the server while maintaining a balance for individual liberty.

<sup>3</sup> At normal times, Administrators are encouraged to maintain close relationships with members of the server.

<sup>4</sup> At times of conflict, Administrators must maintain professionalism and emotional distance.

<sup>5</sup> For any loopholes, flaws, or events not specified in any law or procedures, an Administrator must act in good faith to uphold paragraph 1.

<sup>6</sup> Administrators must fully know the law and specified procedures.

<sup>7</sup> A judging authority must raise the punishment for violations done by Administrators of a senior position.

### Section 2: Server Features

#### Art. 5 Server Settings
The authority to modify any server settings rests only to the Director.

#### Art. 6 Bots
The authority to add, remove, or modify server bots shall rest only to the Head Administrator and Director.

---

## Title 3: Specific Provisions and Compilations

### Section 1: Behaviours

#### Art. 6 Professional Conduct
<sup>1</sup> Administrators are to maintain a respectful manner to all members.

<sup>2</sup> Freedom of speech is limited under Art. 9. of the Public Code (SR 210).

<sup>3</sup> Constant use of slurs, harassment and / or tantrum is punishable by:
a. a warning on the first offence;
b. a 48-hour timeout on the second offence;
c. removal from position on the third offence.

#### Art. 7 Abuse of Power
<sup>1</sup> An Administrator shall not use their powers and act out for personal gain, out of irritation, pettiness, or sheer spite.

<sup>2</sup> Violation of paragraph 1 shall result in:
a. a 48-hour timeout on the first offence; and
b. removal from position on the second offence.

#### Art. 8 Negligence
<sup>1</sup> An Administrator is required to act if available and is well aware a violation of law is happening.

<sup>2</sup> Failure to comply with paragraph 1 shall result in:
a. a warning on the first offence;
b. a 48-hour timeout on the second offence; and
c. removal from position on the third offence.

#### Art. 9 Bribery
Any Administrators that willingly accept any form of compensation by another individual in exchange for administrative influence shall be punished by removal from position and banned from taking any position in Administration.

---

### Section 2: Exercise of Power and Documentation

#### Art. 10 Moderation Actions
<sup>1</sup> Any moderation actions or any general acts shall comply with the Penal Code and the law.

<sup>2</sup> Violation of paragraph 1 shall result in:
a. a warning on the first offence;
b. a 48-hour timeout on the second offence; and
c. removal from position on the third offence.

<sup>3</sup> Other or higher Administrator shall reverse such unlawful moderation decisions and actions immediately.

<sup>4</sup> Actions taken without good faith by administrators that are not explicitly provided for by an enabling law are not exempt from paragraph 1.

#### Art. 11 Documentation of Moderation Actions
<sup>1</sup> All moderation actions are to be documented based on outlined procedures by the Head Administrator or Director.

<sup>2</sup> Failure to log a moderation action shall result in:
a. a warning on the first offence;
b. a 48-hour timeout on the second offence; and
c. removal from position on the third offence.

#### Art. 12 Tampering of Moderation Logs
<sup>1</sup> The tampering, modification to mislead, or deleting moderation logs shall result in:
a. a 48-hour timeout on the first offence;
b. removal from position the second offence.

<sup>2</sup> Moderation logs that are a result of clerical errors is exempt from paragraph 1.
