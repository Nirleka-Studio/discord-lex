# ARCHIVE 200 – 1.1 Lance Administration Reformation Act
*This is an archived rule/law and is no longer in effect.*


```text
1.1
Lance Administration Reformation Act
By the Director
Nirleka Studios, Administration


Article 1. Duty.
Lance Administrators shall have the duty to enforce the Public Code.

Article 2. Enforcement.
Lance Administrators are expected to:
Punish an individual who violated the Public Code accordingly. Method of punishments includes:
(1) A timeout, and
(2) Banishment to The Void;
Announce their enforcement actions stated in Art. 2(a)(1) and (2) to #administration-declarations. This announcement should include:
(1) the Public Code violation(s);
(2) the evidence; and
(3) the punishment and its duration if applicable;
Lift their or other Lance Administrator’s punishments after its stated duration.

Article 3. Balancing Powers
The Secretary or the Head Administrator have the power and duties to:
Lift an unlawful or excessive punishment; and
Interpret the Public Code and determine the appropriate punishment.


```
