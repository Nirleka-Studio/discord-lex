2.1
Penal Code
By the Director
Nirleka Studios, Administration

Preamble
The Penal Code is to maintain consistency for punishing Public Code violations and prevent Lance Administrators and other administrators from punishing members excessively or insufficiently.

Administrators are encouraged to follow these codes with the lens of pragmatism.

Article 1. Definitions.
Administrators are to consult the standard Oxford English dictionary for any undefined words.

Article 2. Common Offenses.
Misusing Channels. Not using channels for their intended purpose is not punishable.
Ping Abuse.
	(i) Ping abuse shall be defined as pinging another member that serves no purpose and is purely intended to annoy or harass.
(1) Mass pinging. Repeatedly pinging another member in large amounts can be punished by a 5 minute timeout upon first offense. Second offenses or more should raise the duration in minutes exponentially.
General NSFW.
(1) In accordance to §2c(a) of the Public Code, medias that explicitly depicts nudity, sexual acts, and the presence of fluids that implies sexual activity shall be deleted immediately and timeout the sender for a minimum of six (6) hours. Repeated offenses shall be punished by multiplying the last timeout duration by two (2). The durations may reset after a week. This includes but not limited to:
	(A) Masturbation even if clothed.
	(B) Images that depicts sexual acts even if cropped.
	(C) Images that depict intentional physical contacts of sex organs or other organs used for sexual pleasure.
	(D) Clothings specifically designed for sexual pleasure AND/OR overexposes the wearer.
	(E) Any imagery that depicts sexual acts among other “species” besides humans.
(2) Medias that depict violent imagery and gore that is intended to shock users shall be deleted immediately and timeout the sender for a minimum of twenty-four (24) hours. Repeated offenses shall raise the timeout duration in hours by multiplying the last timeout duration by two (2). Repeated offenses after three (3) counts should constitute a kick. Repeated offenses after two (2) counts should constitute a ban. This includes but not limited to:
	(A) Gore images intended to shock, even if censored or covered.
(3) All previous punishments does NOT include:
	(A) Real life images that capture such violent imagery, and are intended to encourage discussion. Senders of such images shall be encouraged to use the spoiler tag if not already. If the image is too graphic such as close-up of terminal injuries, depiction of rape, etc. then it is to be deleted immediately and warn the sender.
	(B) Images that certainly depict the character, reference, or originating from a pornography, and does not necessarily depict sexual acts.
	(C) Images that does not necessarily depict sexual acts but is created by a creator famous for creating pornographies. Unless the creator is problematic such as having a history of depicting under-age characters in sexual acts.
	(D) Adult novelties that do not necessarily have the form of a body part or other organs. This includes but is not limited to certain types of vibrators.
	(E) Images that depict surgical operations in a clinical context.
CSAM.
(1) Definition. Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) shall be defined in this server as the visual, digital, or audio documentation of the sexual exploitation or abuse of a real person under the age of consent defined under their own state’s laws. This does not include:
	(A) Fictional drawings not based on real life events or persons.
	(B) AI generated content.
(2) Enforcement.
	(A) Members posting contents defined in Art. 1(d)(1)(A) and (B) shall be punished by timeout for 1 week. Should such members send the same type of content, or worse, again, they shall be banned.
	(B) Members posting contents defined in Art. 1(d)(1) shall be banned immediately.
Advertising or Self-Promotion. (1) Only punishable if:
	(A) The advertising is constant and/or disruptive to other members; and / or
	(B) The member’s sole intention in the server is to advertise.
(2) Enforcement. Members doing Art. 1(e)(1)(A) and (B) are to be asked to stop. If non-compliant, and repeats it, they shall be timed out for two (2) hours. Should the member repeat the action again, they shall be timed out again with the duration of the previous timeout multiplied by two (2). After three counts, they are to be kicked.

Should the member rejoin and repeat the actions again, they shall be banned.
Discord’s Terms of Service. Administrators shall not punish members solely for violating Discord’s ToS unless such violations also violate the Public Code.
Evading punishment through alternative accounts. Any member caught evading punishment such as timeouts, kicks, and bans using an alternative account, the alternative account shall be timed out based on the Administrator’s discretion depending on the severity of the punishment they are trying to evade.
