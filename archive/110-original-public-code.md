```text
From the Director of
NIRLEKA STUDIO
[redacted]

PUBLIC CODE
C. August 16, 2024 L.E. August February 188, 20246

We, the people of Nirleka Studio, and affiliations, in order to form a more perfect environment for our members, contractors, and staff, we hereby declare the establishment of the Public Code.

DEFINITIONS
Roles For the purposes of this document, the roles and titles within the Server will be defined as follows:
Server shall refer to the official Nirleka Studio Discord server.
Member shall refer to an online individual who is verified by an administrative entity and present in the Server.
Administrator shall refer to an online individual who is granted permission of administrative and management access to the Server.
Moderator shall refer to an online individual who is an appointed member of the Server granted by an Administrator responsible for maintaining order, and enforcing community guidelines.
(b) Internet Terminologies Common terminologies and slangs used on the internet.
NSFW stands for “Not Safe For Work” refers to contents, websites, attachments, etc. that are not suitable for viewing in public.
Examples include pornographic, violent and gory imagery. 
NSFL stands for “Not Safe For Life” refers to images and videos that are highly disturbing and can potentially inflict psychological trauma for the viewer.
Examples include the violent, gory torture of a person or animal.

ADMINISTRATION
Collection of User Data Once a user has entered the Server, information, which are user IDs, and messages, regardless if they are edited, or deleted, are collected and logged for moderation purposes.
Such data can not be deleted from Server records even if requested. Exceptions may include:


(i) The Data contains any personal information of individuals.
(ii) The Data contains illegal images of child pornography.

(i) Such redaction of such data is to be logged with texts.

PUBLIC CODES
Public Codes shall apply to all individuals present in the Server, regardless of their verification status.

             § 1b. Public Code — Communication
Members of the Server are to act in a civilised, respectful manner towards other members. 
Instances of disrespect, prejudice, and harassment for other Member’s race, sexual orientation, appearance, or religious beliefs, are prohibited.
Sexual behaviour or references are prohibited.
Members are allowed to express themselves with profanities;
Members however are not allowed to express using any type of slurs;
Members are discouraged to discuss politics or religious beliefs that are expressed in a controversial manner that will lead to serious conflicts;
Members are allowed to share and discuss their own projects, community servers, and others unrelated to Nirleka Studio;
Any communication that directly involves or displays an individual's private personal information, including acts of doxing or the dissemination of such information, is strictly prohibited.
Members are prohibited to engage in any kind of spamming behaviour.

             § 2c. Public Code — Media, Links, and Files
Any media contents, audio, images, GIFs, custom emojis, or stickers, must not contain any NSFW or NSFL content that depicts sexual or violent imagery.
Any image that displays private photos, including an individual's face, maps revealing the private location of a person's home, or images of the home itself, is strictly prohibited. This is in accordance with Section 1b (e) of the Public Code.
Any files that contain malware are prohibited.
Any links that will lead to the following are prohibited:
Any malicious websites, such as phishing, and scam sites;
Pornographic sites;
Sites involving illegal activities and materials;

             § 3c. Public Code — User Accounts and Profiles
Alternatives Accounts are subject to regulations:
The account is not used to impersonate another person;
Bypass bans, either from Discord or the Server;
Used to violate Discord's Terms of Service or the Server's Public Code in any way that protects the original perpetrator.
(b) The appearance of user profiles are subject to regulations:
(1) User’s profile picture and banner must not include an NSFW image. This is
      in accordance with Section 2c (a) of the Public Code.
(2) User’s profile description must not contain any contents that violates
      Section 1b of the Public Code.
```
