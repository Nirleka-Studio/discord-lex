# ARCHIVE 400 – Original Administration Codes
*This is an archived rule/law and is no longer in effect.*



```text
Doc. No. 3
    Of 2026
Administration Code
Compilations
Of 26th April 2026, last edited on the 1st June 2026
______________________________________________________________________________

The Administration Branch of Nirleka Studios Discord Server,
by power given to us as Administrators by the Director, decrees:

Part I. Definitions.

Art. 1. Active voter. Refers to members of the server who have cast a vote within the last 90 days, and all members of the Administration unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Art. 2. Predatory Conduct. Refers to any attempt by a member to establish an inappropriate relationship with a minor or a vulnerable member through deceptive or manipulative means. This includes:
Isolate. Attempting to move conversations to private, unmonitored spaces specifically to bypass community oversight.
Gift or Favour Bribery. Using administrative perks, roles or external gifts to create a sense of obligation or dependency.
Sexualization. Introducing sexual topics or requests for private imagery into a relationship with a minor or subordinate.
Secrecy. Explicitly coaching a member to hide their interactions with the officer from their parents, peers, or other administrators.
Art. 3. Administrator. Refers to:
Lance Administrators;
Senior Administrators;
The Head Administrator; and
The Secretary.
Art. 4. Banishment (to the Void). Refers to the act of removing all the roles of a member and add the role Banished to the Void, causing them to only see the channel #the-void and the #rules channel.
Part II. Amendments.
Art. 1. All members of Administration may request a modification.
Art. 2. The Secretary has the final say on if a modification is lawful or not.
Art. 3. Ideally, the Head Administrator or their stated direct subordinate should make the actual modification to maintain plain, precise, legal, and absolute neutral writing.
Part III. Administration Codes.

Art. 1. Members shall not use an alternative account to add another vote to a vote. Suspected members shall be warned and be told that their vote should be removed. Failure to comply may result in a banishment to the Void for a week. Repeated offenses shall lead to a kick.

Art. 2. Members of Administration shall maintain a respectful manner towards other members. Violation shall result in:
A warn; If previous action still persist in such behavior, punishment shall escalate to:
Timeout for a minimum duration of 24 hours;
Repeat offenses shall double the timeout duration from the previous one;
After three offenses, such behavior shall be informed to the Secretary and request the removal of such member from their position.

Art. 3. An Administrator shall state the reason for the deletion of a message by another member. Such action should be justifiable under the Public or Penal Code. Such administrator not providing a justifiable reason shall be punished by:
A warn; If previous action still persist in such behavior, punishment shall escalate to:
Timeout for a minimum duration of 48 hours;
After a second offense, such behavior shall be informed to the Secretary and request the removal of such member from their position.

Art. 4. Impeachment.

Sec. 1. Definition. An impeachment is the act of accusing and removing a member from Administration for gross violation of the Public and/or Penal Code. This includes but not limited to:
Sending explicit pornographic or violent content; and
Predatory conduct to other members.

Sec. 2. Criteria. A member of Administration can only be impeached as defined in Art. 4(1).

Sec. 3. Procedure. A member of Administration can only be impeached through the following steps:
Any member requests the impeachment of a member of Administration;
Any member of Administration is required to call a public vote, with the options of:
Yes. A member chooses to impeach the individual;
No. A member does not choose to impeach the individual; and
Abstain. The member declines to cast a choice for or against the impeachment.
If two-thirds (2/3) of the total active voters are in favor of impeachment, the individual is impeached and shall be removed from their position; or
If Art.4(3)(c) fails, the individual is not impeached.

Sec. 4. Voting. The individual being impeached cannot cast a vote, if they do their vote is invalid.

Art. 5. Negligence. If an Administrator is fully aware, including actively reading or knowing they receive messages requesting an immediate intervention of a major violation, they shall be punished by:
Warned on the first offense.
Removed from their position after the second offense.

Art. 6. Serving Time of Lance Administrators.
A Lance Administrator can only serve for 6 weeks before a majority vote is called for them to stay in position.
Starting on the 27th of April 2026, all currently serving Lance Administrators have 6 weeks left to serve.

Art. 7. Documentation of Moderation Actions. All Administrators are required to document and log any moderation actions in dedicated channels. This includes but not limited to:
Deletion of messages; and
The Banishment, timeout, kick, or ban of a member.

Art. 8. The power to add and remove server bots rests with the Head Administrator and their delegated staff.

Art. 9. Any members of Administration that will willingly accept any form of compensation by another individual in exchange of administrative influence shall be punished by removal from position and banned from taking any position in Administration.

Art. 10. The deliberate deletion or tampering of logs shall be punishable by timeout with the duration of 7 days. Should such a user commit a second offense, they shall be permanently removed from their position.

Art. 11. Celebration of Certain Events.
Administrators or any members that manages events shall not organize, host, or promote any events, or programming centered around recognized holidays and cultural observances (including, but not limited to, religious, federal, state, or national holidays, and designated heritage or awareness months) that is not included in the following section (b).
Permitted events includes:
Easter;
Halloween;
Thanksgiving;
Christmas;
Eid al-Fitr;
Eid al-Adha;
Valentines Day;
Mother’s Day;
Father’s Day;
New Year;
Any country’s celebration on workers’ rights; and
Any country's Independence Day or Victory Day.
The addition or removal of any items in section (b) can only be permitted by a server-wide vote with a two-thirds majority.

Art. 12. An Administrator cannot abuse their power for personal gain, out of irritation, pettiness, and / or sheer spite. Punishment shall be a 24 hour time out. And they shall be removed from their position on the second offense.
Part IV. The Executive Branch.

Art. 1. Composition and General Duties.
The Executive Branch shall be responsible for the enforcement of the Public and Penal Code, maintenance of order, and the general administration of the server. All members of the Executive Branch are bound by the bylaws and may not exercise powers beyond those explicitly granted to their respective role. The Executive Branch shall consist of following ranks:
Lance Administrator;
Senior Administrator, and
The Head Administrator.

Art. 2. Lance Administrator.

Sec. 1. Role. The Lance Administrator is the entry-level enforcement role of the executive branch. Lance Administrators serve as the first line of moderation and are expected to be active, present, and responsive to member conduct.

Sec. 2. Powers. Lance Administrators shall have the power to:
Issue timeouts and warns to members as stated in the Penal Code and this code;
Banish members to the Void;
Delete messages that violate the Public or Penal Code; and
Kick members from the server.
Sec. 3. Limitations. Lance Administrators shall not have the power to:
Issue temporary or permanent bans;
Override or reverse the decisions of Senior or Head Administrators;
Add or remove roles from a member, including themselves, that are not necessary for the enforcement of the Public, Penal, or this Administration Codes or not authorised by Senior Administrators or the Head Administrator.

Art. 3. Senior Administrator.

Sec. 1. Role. The Senior Administrator is the intermediate enforcement role of the executive branch. Senior Administrators have the duty to:
Supervise Lance Administrators;
Punish them for violations according to the Public, Penal, and this Code;
Handle escalated moderation matters; and
Carry expanded enforcement authority granted by the Head Administrator.

Sec. 2. Powers.
All powers granted to Lance Administrators;
Issue temporary or permanent bans;
Review and reverse moderation decisions made by Lance Administrators; and
May appoint a Lance Administrator if members have voted so.
Sec. 3. Limitations. Senior Administrators shall not have the power to:
Unilaterally appoint or demote members of the executive branch;
Reverse decisions made by the Head Administrator; and
Act on disputes in which they are personally involved.

Art. 4. Head Administrator.

Sec. 1. Role. The Head Administrator is the highest authority within the executive branch. The Head Administrator has the ultimate responsibility for the conduct of the executive branch, the integrity of enforcement, and the structural operation of the server.

Sec. 2. Powers.
All powers granted to Lance and Senior Administrators;
Appoint, demote, suspend, or remove members of the executive branch;
Overturn or uphold any moderation decision made by any executive branch member; and
Issue binding directives to the executive branch.

Art. 5. Electing Lance Administrators.
Sec. 1. Election Day. Starting from the 27th of April 2026, for every 6 weeks, Lance Administrators who have served for 6 weeks shall be removed from their position if they are not currently managing a conflict. If they are, they can be removed if another Administrator can take care of it.
Sec. 2. Preparation. Before the election day, preferably a week before, the Secretary shall compile a list of candidates. With the criterias of:
They must be active on the server;
Have done zero intentional or reckless violations of the Public, Penal, and this Administration Codes;
Shall not have already served as Lance Administrator for more than 2 terms and;
They want to serve in the first place.
Sec. 3. Voting. After all Lance Administrators who have served their time have been removed accordingly, a vote shall be called, with a minimum duration of three (3) days, where the options are the list of candidates defined in Sec. 2.
Only a maximum of two (2) candidates can be voted.
Members of Administration or their candidates themselves cannot cast a vote. Otherwise it is invalid.
Sec. 4. Finalisation. After the vote is finished, if a candidate has the majority vote, they shall be promoted as Lance Administrator.

Part V. Temporary Roles.

Art. 1. The Secretary.

Sec. 1. Powers The Secretary shall have the power to:
Form a body within Administration and give them the powers to enforce the Public, Penal, and this Administration Codes for server members and members of Administration;
Appoint someone to said body; and
Dissolve said body.
```
