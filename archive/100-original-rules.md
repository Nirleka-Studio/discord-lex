# ARCHIVE 100 – Original Server Rules
*This is an archived rule/law and is no longer in effect.*


```markdown
<:Rules:1115263355989528626>  **Server Rules**
# 📜 **Standard Rules**
Rules that will be enforced with appropriate punishments for violations.

🚫 **No Spamming**
Do not engage in any form of spamming. Violating this rule will result in a temporary mute for a duration of 2-5 minutes, depending on the severity.

🔇 **No Controversial Topics**
Avoid discussing controversial subjects that may cause tension or conflict among members.
<:list_bottom:1117803557513613502>  **07-1 Exception: Controversial Discussion are only allowed in a controled manner, and supervised by server administrators.**

💼 **Advertisements**
While sharing something like YouTube videos, discord servers, etc is allowed, refrain from excessive self-promotion that may disrupt other members' experience. Violations will result in a 2-minute mute.

# 📜 **Critical Rules**
Rules that, if violated, will lead to severe consequences.

🚫 No Harassment, Bullying, or Unpleasant Behavior
Any form of harassment, bullying, or behavior that creates an unpleasant environment will not be tolerated. Victims are encouraged to report incidents to the moderators, and violators will face permanent bans.

Swearing is allowed. Just do not use it as an offense. Slurs are also not tolerated.

🚫 No NSFW Content
Most members in this server are under 18 or may be traumatized if exposed to NSFW content. Please refrain from sharing sexual content, gore, and so on.
📝 This also includes sexual behavior.
📝 And sexual references.

🚫 No Malicious Alt Accounts
Creating alternative accounts without malicious intent is permitted. However, impersonation is strictly prohibited.

# 📜 **Policy Rules**
Rules aimed at guiding new members, with no punishment.

👥 **Reporting Violations**
Please promptly report any rule violations to the moderators. If you feel offended, bullied, or encounter any inappropriate content, capture the messages and submit a report.

📻  **Channels**
Please use channels for their intended purposes. 
@everyone
```
