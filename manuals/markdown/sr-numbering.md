## 1.1 Temporary decrees
Temporary decrees starts with `0` and the number based on chronology.

e.g.

`SR 310.01`: Fixes or clarifies an Article in the Penal Code.

## 1.2 Repeals
A law's slot is permanently reserved even if it is repealed.
